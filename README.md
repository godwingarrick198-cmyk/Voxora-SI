# Voxora AI

AI receptionist SaaS for businesses. Initial focus: inbound-call handling, lead capture, and follow-up workflows.

## Build principles
- Keep the product separate from CHM SMART ATTEND projects.
- Prefer free tiers and avoid paid services unless explicitly approved.
- Never claim live phone calling works until a real provider integration has been configured and tested.

## Planned MVP
- Business accounts and authentication
- Receptionist configuration by business and industry
- Lead capture and lead management
- Call history and outcomes
- Integration-ready inbound voice webhook
- Responsive dashboard

## Status
Project initialized. Voice-provider setup and database configuration are not yet complete.
